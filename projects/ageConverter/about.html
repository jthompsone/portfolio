About

This is a fantacy ancestry reletive age converter. It takes an imput ancestry and converts that age to what would be relative age of another ancestry. You can also use this calculator for real world animals and humans -- just create a custom list, download the file, and upload it back to the calculator.

By default, the age converter will load a list of predefined ancestries.

To make a custom list of ancestries, use the Creator tool, and then on the Calculator page, select "Load Custom List".
