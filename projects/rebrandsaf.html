Save A Fox - Rebrand

As an exercise, I tried my hand at rebranding a website. I chose a non-profit animal rescue/sancuatry Save A Fox, where they rescue foxes from fur farms and other situations. Their current website is decent, but I felt could be more lively and reflect the values of Save A Fox. I wouldn't code this website, but rather just design it in Figma.

Wireframing

One of the first things I did starting into the project was to design wireframes for the new website rebrand.

Ideas

I came up with two directions this rebrand could go, and wound up choosing the second idea, as I felt it more reflected Save A Fox's personality of open and playful. I would however, wind up keeping both idea's key visuals, as I felt both were great and felt inline with the theme I was going for.
